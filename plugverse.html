The problem

Every gig starts from scratch. Bio rewritten. Setlist re-attached. Rate re-explained. Contract copy-pasted from a Google Doc. There's no LinkedIn for local music. No Airbnb listing for "here's who I am, what I charge, when I'm free."

The analogy

Airbnb.

Rooms existed before Airbnb. Bookings existed before Airbnb. What didn't exist was the layer that made it standard. Listings, identity, payments, reviews.

Local music has the same gap. Plugverse is the layer.

What it is

A three-sided
marketplace.

Artists list their availability and rate. Venues post their stages and dates. Organizers find the right act and book it in one flow. Contract auto-generated. Payment in escrow until the show is done.

Artists

One profile, every gig

Bio, setlist, rate, samples, dates available. Send the link instead of the twenty-minute pitch.

Venues

Fill the calendar

Post the room, the date, the budget. Discoverable to every artist on the platform.

Organizers

Book the show end-to-end

Search, offer, sign, pay. One flow. Free to use. Contract and escrow handled for you.

How a booking happens

Organizer side.

Recording from the live UI. An organizer drafts a direct offer to an artist with performance time, arrival time, lineup role, and budget. The contract auto-fills from the artist's profile. Sign, hold the payment in escrow, done.

  • One offer flow for any gig type
  • Auto-generated contract from artist + venue defaults
  • Payment held in escrow until the show is performed
