The problem

A local band gets a text. You guys available April 12th? They spend twenty minutes writing a bio they have written forty times. Attach a setlist from the camera roll. Copy a contract from a Google Doc they found in 2023. Send their Venmo. Two weeks later, same thing. Different venue. Same twenty minutes.

The problem is not payment. Artists get paid. The problem is that there is no professional identity layer for independent artists. No persistent profile. No searchable availability. No standard contract. Every booking is cold and manual and starts at zero.

What I built

One profile that does the work for them. Get discovered. Get booked. Contract auto-generated. Payment held in escrow until the gig is done. Free for organizers. Marketplace fee on artist payout. Built with Stripe Connect, Supabase, Next.js, and a lot of late nights.
