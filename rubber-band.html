07Live shows Tap any tile

Shows
I've caught.

Every show is research. The way a crowd reacts to a transition. How the band paces the set. What the venue does to a sound. Notes for our own room.

Grunge / Stadium
Pearl Jam
May 13, 2025
Pearl Jam
Dark Matter Tour

Two encores. The room dimmed to a single light on "Black." Thirty-five years deep and Vedder still has the back of every front-row seat in the building.

Anthem rock / Spectacle
Coldplay
Summer 2024
Coldplay
Music of the Spheres

Synchronized wristbands turned the stadium into one organism. Cried at "Fix You" — won't pretend otherwise. Best argument I've seen for the case that pop scale is its own art form.

Rock / Three-hour set
Foo Fighters
Summer 2024
Foo Fighters
PNC Pavilion · Charlotte

Three hours, no opener. Grohl pulled a kid up to play "My Hero" and didn't lower the bar. Whole arena lost the second half of "Best Of You."

Indie folk / Mid-set quiet
The Backseat Lovers
Fall 2024
The Backseat Lovers
The Fillmore · Charlotte

The kind of band that earns the mid-set quiet. The Fillmore packed wall to wall, everyone singing "Kilby Girl" loud enough that the band could've stopped playing.

Country folk / Outdoor
Zach Bryan
Summer 2024
Zach Bryan
Credit One Stadium · Charleston

Charleston heat, the "Something in the Orange" sing-along, and 30,000 people in cowboy boots remembering they could feel something. Lowcountry sunset doing half the work.

Indie pop / Folk
Vance Joy
Spring 2024
Vance Joy
The Fillmore · Charlotte

"Riptide" hits different in a 2,000-cap room. The crowd finished the chorus for him; he just smiled and let it happen.

Funk rock / First stadium
Red Hot Chili Peppers
2023
Red Hot Chili Peppers
BofA Stadium · Charlotte

First stadium show I ever saw. Frusciante back. "Snow" into "Otherside" — the bassline that's the reason I picked up the instrument.

Bedroom pop / R&B
Keshi
2023
Keshi
The Fillmore · Charlotte

Dream-pop ceiling, the venue washed in red. Hyperreal version of the records — you could feel the bedroom-pop production filling out into something arena-shaped.

Alt rock / Direct influence
Smashing Pumpkins
2024
Smashing Pumpkins
World is a Vampire Tour

Direct influence on the Cooper Delo sound. Billy Corgan still impossible to look away from. "1979" through a wall of cabs is its own genre.
