01Manifesto

I shoot what I want to remember. The songs, the places, the rooms before they emptied. Same instinct that ships the apps. Capture it while it's hot. Cut it before it cools. Run it back.

A lens is just a tool for paying attention. The phone in my pocket, the Canon for the slow stuff, the playlist that scored every drive. This page is the visual half of the same loop running everywhere else.
