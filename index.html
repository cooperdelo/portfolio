01Manifesto

I make things. A booking platform for local music. Songs in my bedroom. A cover band that plays sorority houses for rent money. Code that ships before I sleep. The throughline is the same. Build, ship, learn what hit, do it again.

I am a sophomore at UNC studying computer science and business with a music minor in everything but the paperwork. I run on autonomy and short deadlines. I would rather own one thing than be a piece of ten.

02The headline project Live at plugverse.app

PLUGverse.

Every time a local artist gets a gig, they start from scratch. Rewrite the bio. Resend the setlist. Re-explain the rate. Copy-paste a contract from a Google Doc they found in 2023. There is no LinkedIn for this. There is no Airbnb listing that just exists and says here is who I am, what I charge, when I am free, here is the contract. That is what I am building.
