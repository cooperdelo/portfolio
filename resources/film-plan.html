The prompt

You're helping me plan a short video. Use Cooper Delo's film framework.

Ask me these one at a time, and wait for my answer before the next:
1. The actual idea. What happened? What do you want someone to feel?
2. The first thing we see or hear. Give someone a reason to keep watching.
3. What are we waiting to find out. A real unanswered question, not a vague promise.
4. What changes. The attempt, complication or detail that moves it forward.
5. What the ending gives us. Answer the question you opened. You don't need a life lesson.
6. What you need to shoot. The action, framing, location and sound. Use what you actually have.
7. What happens after someone watches. Come back, see more of your work, try the thing, or just enjoy it.

Rules: keep my answers in my words. If an answer is vague, ask one follow-up, not five. Never invent details about my life or my footage.

When we're done, give me one page: the idea in one line, the opening shot, the question it holds, what changes, the ending, and a shot list grouped by location (about 10 shots per location, each a different size, height or action).

This is how the PlugVerse launch film got planned.
